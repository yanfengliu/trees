## The panel's own key handling made the "ignore keys while typing" check vacuous

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-25 02:50–03:20

**Action:** `src/ui/cameraControls.ts` listens for `keydown`/`keyup` on `window` in the capture phase and skips keys whose target is a text field (`isTypingTarget` in `src/ui/movement.ts`). `scripts/shots.mjs --input` clicks a panel field, holds W for 0.5 s and asserts the camera did not move.

**Result:** With bubbling listeners the check passed even with `isTypingTarget` forced to `false`: lil-gui calls `stopPropagation()` on every key event inside the panel (`lil-gui.esm.js` lines 89–90), so the guard was never exercised. After switching to capture listeners the same mutation fails the check (moved 1.714 m), and the real code passes (0.000 m).

**Reasoning:** Capture is needed for a second reason: a key released while a panel field has focus must still clear the held key, or the camera keeps moving forever. Bubbling `keyup` would be swallowed by lil-gui.

**Validation:** Mutation run above. It would still miss focus moving to some other element that does not stop propagation and is not a text field; there is none on the page today.

**Code reviewer comments:** None yet; found by reintroducing the defect, not by review.

## Headless frame rate made a fixed settle wait flaky

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-25 03:20–03:35

**Action:** `--input` now waits until the mirrored pose (`canvas[data-pose]`) is unchanged over 300 ms instead of a fixed 0.7–1 s sleep.

**Result:** A fixed wait left 0.108 m of residual wheel velocity in the next step on a loaded 4-core machine and failed it; with the stability wait every step reads 0.000 m of drift.

**Reasoning:** `main.ts` clamps `dt` to 0.1 s, so below 10 fps simulated time runs slower than wall time and damping takes longer in wall time than the constants suggest.

**Validation:** After the stability wait, one run in six failed a step that was not logged (the run was read by its summary line only); the movement thresholds were then lowered to 0.3 m forward for a 1 s W press and 0.2 m up for a 1 s E press, and the next three runs were green. Speeds measured headless (1.6–3.7 m for a 1 s W press) prove direction, not feel at 60 fps. The unexplained failure is open.
